Responsible farming, stated plainly

These are the practices we operate today. We publish what we measure and avoid claims we cannot support.

Animal welfare

Housing, stocking density, handling and veterinary care set as standing routine rather than exception.

Sustainable feed practice

Feed sourced and mixed to reduce waste, with usage recorded against each production unit.

Waste management

Litter and processing residue handled through defined routes, returned to land where appropriate.

Soil protection

Cover management and fertilisation planned to maintain plantation soil condition over successive cycles.

Water management

Controlled supply for livestock and irrigation, with drainage managed across the plantation.

Local employment

Farm labour, training and procurement drawn from surrounding communities.
